How shorts-forge works

One command per video (or a whole folder). Every stage has an offline fallback.
1 · input

Long video

Podcast, interview, stream or lecture. Any size ffmpeg can read. Batch: many files at once.

ffprobe → size, fps, audio

2 · transcribe

Word timestamps

faster-whisper locally, or any OpenAI-compatible STT API, or a saved transcript JSON.

transcript.json

3 · sentences

Clip-safe units

Words regrouped on punctuation and pauses, so a short never starts or ends mid-word.

4 · pick highlights

Hook scoring

LLM (OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama, LM Studio, Anthropic) returns start/end sentence ids, title, hook score 0-100, reason. Output is validated: ids clamped, durations fixed, overlaps removed.
Heuristic (no key): questions, numbers, "you", curiosity words, filler and pace.

5 · reframe 9:16

Face tracking

OpenCV samples 4 frames/s; YuNet (bundled 230 KB ONNX) or Haar finds the speaker. Gap fill, median + EMA smoothing, a dead zone so the camera holds still, then Douglas-Peucker keyframes → one ffmpeg crop expression. No face: center crop or blurred fit.

6 · captions

Animated ASS

2-4 word phrases, active word coloured and popped. Styles: bold, karaoke, clean. RTL (Persian/Arabic) supported.

7 · render

One ffmpeg pass

Cut + crop + scale + libass burn + H.264/AAC, 1080×1920, faststart. Thumbnail per clip.

8 · output

Ranked shorts

01-why-do-90-percent.mp4
01-why-do-90-percent.ass
01-why-do-90-percent.jpg
manifest.json

manifest.json records every decision: cut points, hook score and reason, picker, detector and crop keyframes.
